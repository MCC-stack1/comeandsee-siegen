2. In short

This website provides information about the community “Come & See”. We want to process as little data as possible. That is why:

  • We use no cookies and store nothing on your device.
  • We use no analytics or tracking services and no advertising.
  • There is no contact form, no registration and no user account.
  • We embed no external content (no videos, no maps, no social media plugins). Fonts and QR codes are also provided without any connection to third parties: the fonts are stored on our own server, and the QR codes are generated directly in your browser.

3. Hosting and server log files

This website is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. The provider is Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. Cloudflare processes data on our behalf (data processing under Art. 28 GDPR).

When you visit the website, data that your browser sends to the server is automatically transmitted and processed for technical reasons. This includes in particular:

  • IP address,
  • date and time of access,
  • the page or file requested and the server’s response (status code, data volume),
  • browser type, operating system and the page visited before (referrer), if your browser sends them.

Purpose and legal basis: The processing is necessary to deliver the website to you and to ensure its secure and stable operation. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in the secure and efficient operation of the website). We do not combine this data with other data or draw conclusions about your person.

Storage period: We do not store access data ourselves. The storage period at Cloudflare is governed by Cloudflare’s own policies.

Transfer to the USA: To our knowledge, Cloudflare is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. In addition, Cloudflare provides for the EU Commission’s standard contractual clauses. More information: cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

The connection to this website is encrypted using TLS (https).
