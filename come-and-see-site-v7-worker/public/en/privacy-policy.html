2. In short

This website provides information about the community “Come & See”. We want to process as little data as possible. That is why:

  • We use no cookies and store nothing on your device.
  • We use no analytics or tracking services and no advertising.
  • There is no contact form, no registration and no user account.
  • When you visit, your browser makes no connection to Google, Instagram or other third parties apart from our hosting provider (see section 3). There are no embedded videos, maps or social media plugins. The fonts are stored on our own server, the QR codes are generated directly in your browser, the events are delivered by our own server (see section 4), and Instagram is only linked.

3. Hosting and server log files

This website is hosted on Cloudflare (Cloudflare Workers). The provider is Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. Cloudflare processes data on our behalf (data processing under Art. 28 GDPR).

When you visit the website, data that your browser sends to the server is automatically transmitted and processed for technical reasons. This includes in particular:

  • IP address,
  • date and time of access,
  • the page or file requested and the server’s response (status code, data volume),
  • browser type, operating system and the page visited before (referrer), if your browser sends them.

Purpose and legal basis: The processing is necessary to deliver the website to you and to ensure its secure and stable operation. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in the secure and efficient operation of the website). We do not combine this data with other data or draw conclusions about your person.

Storage period: We do not store access data ourselves. The storage period at Cloudflare is governed by Cloudflare’s own policies.

Transfer to the USA: To our knowledge, Cloudflare is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. In addition, Cloudflare provides for the EU Commission’s standard contractual clauses. More information: cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

The connection to this website is encrypted using TLS (https).

4. Event calendar

We maintain the events in the “Events” section in a publicly viewable Google Calendar. They are not retrieved from Google by your browser but by a function on our own hosting (Cloudflare, see section 3). This function requests the events via Google’s calendar interface (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland) and passes them on to your browser. Only the public event data (title, date, time, place, description) is requested. Your IP address and other data from your visit are not transmitted to Google. The events are cached for up to five minutes so that they do not have to be requested anew with every visit.

Purpose and legal basis: Providing an up-to-date overview of events, Art. 6 (1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in informing visitors about our gatherings).

If you click “Open in Google Calendar” or “Subscribe to calendar”, you leave our website. From then on, Google’s privacy policy applies.

  • Instagram (Meta Platforms Ireland Limited, Merrion Road, Dublin 4, D04 X2K5, Ireland): Privacy policy

The “Copy address” function only places the address on your device’s clipboard. No data is sent to us or to third parties.

If you join the WhatsApp community or follow us on Instagram, we process your details there (e.g. name or profile name) in the course of communication in the group. The rules of the respective service apply; we do not pass your data on.

To exercise your rights, please write to the email address given above.

Right to lodge a complaint: You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR). The authority responsible for us is the Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Nordrhein-Westfalen (State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of North Rhine-Westphalia), Kavalleriestraße 2–4, 40213 Düsseldorf, Germany, www.ldi.nrw.de. You can also contact the authority at your place of residence.
